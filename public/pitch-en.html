03 / The JuLit SolutionSamuel Paredes · 1:00–1:30

Payment and lot delivery,
in the exact same transaction.

JuLit closes the risk window around a digital title per lot, enforced by a Delivery-versus-Payment (DvP) protocol on Solana.

01 / TOKENIZATION

Title in Escrow

The lot is created reserved to a designated buyer: the Digital Title (NFT) is minted directly into protocol escrow.

02 / ESCROW DEPOSIT

Payment in custody

The designated buyer deposits the purchase price in USDC into the PDA escrow (fund_lot). Funds locked securely.

03 / ATOMIC REDEMPTION

Atomic settlement

Upon physical delivery, the buyer signs redeem_lot: at that exact moment the producer gets paid and the title is burned.

RESERVED TRADES & SAFEGUARDSNo open storefront: only the designated buyer can settle. If the buyer is unresponsive, the producer collects via timeout (claim_timeout); in case of non-conformity, the escrow freezes (raise_dispute).

Physical transport and customs clearance happen outside the protocol. The digital title never enters an external wallet: it lives and dies inside the escrow.
