01 / The Problem 2 / 9

New producers sell to buyers they don’t know.

Big lithium companies sell to their own partners. New, small companies have no partner — and no safe way to get paid.

Big players

Partners

Cauchari-Olaroz (Jujuy): its owners take the lithium in proportion to their shares.1 No trust problem to solve.

New players

100%

Argosy Minerals (Salta) sold 20 t to a Hong Kong buyer, who paid everything before the ship was loaded.2

Banks

45%

of trade finance rejections go to SMEs, which send only 38% of the requests.3

A letter of credit is expensive and needs a lot of paperwork.4 Of rejected SMEs that looked for other financing, 40% used their own money5 — with no protection.

03 / The Solution 4 / 9

A payment box that opens only on delivery.

01 / REGISTER

Producer profile

We register each producer with its production data, like lithium purity. Every lot uses the same data.

02 / CONTRACT

Lot for one buyer

Producer and buyer sign a contract in JuLit. The producer creates a lot for that buyer.

03 / DEPOSIT

Money locked

The buyer puts the money, in USDC, into the box. Nobody can take it — not even us.

04 / DELIVERY

Paid on confirm

The lithium arrives, the buyer checks it and confirms. At that moment, the producer gets paid.

IF THE BUYER DOES NOT ANSWER The producer can collect the money after a set date, agreed in the contract.
04 / Why Solana 5 / 9

The rules run by themselves.

One

A program, not a person

The box is a program on Solana. It follows the rules by itself. No bank or company holds the money.

Two

All in one step

The buyer’s confirmation and the payment to the producer happen together, in one transaction. All or nothing.

Three

Proof of History

It works like a shared clock: a proof of the order of events and the time between them.7 Everyone sees the same order — first the deposit, then the payment.

Four

Cheap fees

Each transaction costs less than one cent8, so it works for every lot, even small ones.

05 / Business Model 6 / 9

We earn only when the payment is released.

Take rate < 1%

A fee under 1 percent, charged only when the box releases the money to the producer. Creating a lot and depositing are free.

Why they pay Safety

The producer ships knowing the money is already locked. The buyer pays first — as small buyers already do without a bank — but now the money is protected until delivery.

We don’t replace bank credit. We give a safe option to the companies that can’t get it.
