Safe payments for new lithium companies

A B2B directory and payment box on Solana. Built in Jujuy, Argentina.

The problem

Hi, we are JuLit, from Jujuy, Argentina.

Big lithium companies sell to their own partners.1 They don't need us.

But new, small producers often sell to buyers they don't know.

For example, Argosy Minerals, in Salta, sold 20 tonnes to a buyer in Hong Kong. The buyer paid everything before the ship was loaded.2 All the risk was on the buyer.

Why not use a bank? A letter of credit is expensive and needs a lot of paperwork.3 And banks reject small companies more often.4 Many of them end up paying with their own money, with no protection.5

Our solution — live demo

JuLit makes these sales safe, with a payment box on Solana.

[Show screen: Explorer → producer profile]

First, we register each producer with its production data, like lithium purity. Every lot uses the same data.

[Show screen: Producer creates a lot]

Second, producer and buyer sign a contract in JuLit, and the producer creates a lot for that buyer.

[Show screen: Buyer deposits USDC]

Third, the buyer puts the money, in USDC, into the box. Nobody can take it — not even us.

Yes, the buyer pays first. But without a bank, small buyers already pay first. With JuLit, that money is protected.

[Show screen: Buyer confirms delivery → payment]

Finally, the lithium arrives. The buyer checks it and confirms. At that moment, the producer gets paid.

Why Solana

We use Solana because it is fast, and each transaction costs less than one cent.6

Business model, status and team

Our business model: we charge a 1 percent fee, only when the payment is released.

Everything in the demo is real, on Solana Devnet, with test money.

And we live in Jujuy, in the Lithium Triangle, close to the new producers.
