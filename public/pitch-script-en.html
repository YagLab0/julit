JuLit
Pitch · English

Safe payments for new lithium companies

An escrow on Solana for lithium sales between new producers and buyers they don't know. Built in Jujuy, Argentina. Recorded on camera, with short screen cuts taken from the devnet demo recording.

1 · The problem · 0:00–0:35

We are JuLit — four builders from Jujuy, Argentina, inside the Lithium Triangle.

Big lithium companies sell to their own partners.1 But new, small producers sell to buyers they don't know.

Argosy Minerals, in Salta, sold 20 tonnes to a buyer in Hong Kong. The buyer paid everything before the ship was loaded.2 All the risk was on the buyer.

A letter of credit is expensive and slow,3 and banks reject small companies more often.4 So many of them pay with their own money, with no protection.5

2 · The solution · 0:35–1:05

JuLit is an escrow on Solana for these sales.

[Cut 1 · Producer creates a lot · ~7 s]

The producer creates a lot for one buyer, with a ship-by date and a window to confirm.

[Cut 2 · Buyer deposits dUSDC · ~7 s]

The buyer deposits USDC into the escrow.

[Cut 3 · Producer marks shipped · ~7 s]

The producer ships, and records the hash of the bill of lading on-chain.

[Cut 4 · Buyer confirms → producer paid · ~7 s]

The buyer checks the lithium and confirms. The producer gets paid.

3 · Why it is safe · 1:05–1:30

Nobody gets stuck. If the producer never ships, the buyer gets a full refund. If the buyer never answers, the producer can claim the payment.

The fee is frozen on-chain when the buyer deposits, and it can never be more than 2 percent.

[Cut 5 · Solana Explorer, fund_lot and redeem_lot txs · label "devnet · test dUSDC" · ~6 s]

Each step costs less than one cent.6 It runs today on Solana devnet. These are the real transactions.

4 · Business & validation · 1:30–1:45

We charge 1 percent, only when the payment is settled.

5 · Next & close · 1:45–2:00

Next: our first pilot lot with a producer from northern Argentina.

With JuLit, the buyer is protected if nothing ships, and the producer ships knowing the money is there. We are JuLit. Thank you.
